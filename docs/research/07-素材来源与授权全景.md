# 07 · 素材来源与授权全景：一期记载勘误、可用源、真人面孔边界与面孔表征证据

- **调研日期**：2026-09-23 ～ 2026-09-24
- **调研用途**：为 T3 素材管线回炉（ADR-0007 待定）提供一手核实的素材授权底图：哪些现成源可直接入库、哪些只能当风格参考、哪些一律不碰；并勘误一期文档中与一手来源不符的记载。
- **检索口径**：只认一手来源（许可证原文、仓库 LICENSE/README、官网使用条款、法规原文、论文原文或 PubMed/PMC/Europe PMC、实际请求过的 API URL）。条款引用带条款号与原文短引；**〔推断〕** 标出非原文结论。AI 生图合规不在本文范围（见报告 10）。
- **性质说明**：案头调研笔记，不构成法律意见。

---

## 给决策者的摘要（≤300 字）

**与既有记载的冲突共 6 处**（§一）：①词库、ADR-0002、总体规划把真人照片当素材——已核 7 个真人表情库 6 个限科研，唯一 CC BY 的仅成人、仅中性与微笑，儿童照片另受 PIPL 与肖像权约束，真人档只走家长本地导入；②ARASAAC 应为 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0，且不得与 BY-SA 素材合成；③核查清单漏列 unDraw 等三家插画站；④研 02 对 CAFE"多为仅限科研"的判断方向正确，应收紧为"已核者全部"，并提防把 CAFE 页面上两处 CC BY 误读为图像许可（2026-09-27 更正：本条初稿误写成"研 02 误把 CC BY 当图像许可"，研 02 原文并无此误）；⑤Mulberry 数量与署名过时；⑥Twemoji 版权行不符上游。新增可入库：Fluent Emoji、FxEmoji、Open Peeps，及须预生成的 DiceBear Moods。同龄、本族面孔仅弱证据；中文原生开放图卡未找到。

### A1　"真人照片素材"作为泛化机制示例

- 可随开源仓库再分发的真人表情库，本轮一个也没找到：逐库条款见 §三.1。唯一 CC BY 4.0 的 Face Research Lab London Set 仅含 102 名成人，文件只有 `neutral_*` 与 `smiling_*` 两类（[figshare API 文件清单](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/5047666/files)）。
- 儿童面孔还受中国法约束（§三.2：个人信息保护法、民法典直接适用，人脸识别司法解释作参照）；即使拿到合规授权，公开 git 仓库也难以兑现"撤回同意 → 删除"〔推断，§三.3〕。
- 可行的"真人"形态只剩两种：家长在设备上本地导入自家照片、家长真人示范（共玩回合）——核查清单 §三.3 已按此落地，与 ADR-0004 零收集架构一致。
- **与已定案记载的冲突（明说，不静默覆盖）**：ADR-0002（accepted）把"真人照片素材"列为泛化机制示例，总体规划 T3 写"真人表情开源数据集"。建议在 ADR-0007 中把示例改为"家长本地导入的真人照片 / 家长真人示范"，并同步消歧 `CONTEXT.md` 词条。本文不改动 ADR 与词库。

### A2　ARASAAC × OpenMoji：并列可以，合成不行

1. **许可版本**：arasaac.org 前端源码的许可徽标组件链接 `https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/deed.${locale}`（[CreativeCommons.js](https://github.com/juanda99/arasaac-frontend/blob/master/app/components/License/CreativeCommons.js)；该仓库描述为 "New website for arasaac.org"，线上 [arasaac.org/terms-of-use](https://arasaac.org/terms-of-use) 是 JS 壳页，无法直接读取正文）。旧站条款页（[Wayback 2019-01-08 快照](https://web.archive.org/web/20190108042733/http://arasaac.org/condiciones_uso.php)）链接的是 `creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/`——**版本曾由 3.0 升为 4.0**，素材入库时应按下载时点的版本登记。
2. **ARASAAC 条款原文**（[messages.js](https://github.com/juanda99/arasaac-frontend/blob/master/app/components/License/messages.js)，最后修改 2023-10-05）："authorizing their use for non-profit purposes providing the source and the author, and are shared under the same license"；"Therefore, the use of these resources within any product or publication for commercial purposes is excluded."；"Any work derived from the resources contained in the {ARASAAC} catalogs … must be distributed with the same {creativeCommonsLicense}"。署名要素为作者 Sergio Palao、所有者 Gobierno de Aragón、来源 ARASAAC、许可名称，与核查清单现有署名格式一致。
3. **合成为何违约**（CC 4.0 法律文本原文）：
   - §1(a) Adapted Material：Licensed Material 被 "translated, altered, arranged, transformed, or otherwise modified in a manner requiring permission" 而形成的材料。
   - [BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en) §3(b)(1)："The Adapter's License You apply must be a Creative Commons license with the same License Elements, this version or later, or a BY-SA Compatible License."；§1 定义："The License Elements of this Public License are Attribution and ShareAlike."
   - [BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode.en) §3(b)(1)：同一句式，但 License Elements 为 "Attribution, NonCommercial, and ShareAlike"，兼容项为 "BY-NC-SA Compatible License"。
   - 两份许可的 §3(b)(3) 均为："You may not offer or impose any additional or different terms or conditions on, or apply any Effective Technological Measures to, Adapted Material that restrict exercise of the rights granted under the Adapter's License You apply."；§2(a)(5)(C) "No downstream restrictions" 对原素材有同样的禁止。
   - 结论〔推断，与下条 CC 官方口径一致〕：一张同时含 BY-SA 与 BY-NC-SA 素材的改编图，找不到能同时满足两份 §3(b)(1) 的改编者许可；按 BY-NC-SA 发布，等于给 BY-SA 素材加了"非商业"限制。
   - CC 官方口径：[CC FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/)："if you create a remix with material licensed under a ShareAlike license, you need to make sure that all of the material contributed to the remix is licensed under the same license or one that CC has named as compatible"；[CC wiki 兼容性页](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/Wiki/cc_license_compatibility)（最后编辑 2013-05-31）："It is not possible to mix works where the first work is placed under a Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike license and the second work is licensed under a Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike license."
4. **并列放置不受影响**：CC FAQ："If the combination does not create an adaptation, then you may combine any CC-licensed content so long as you provide attribution and comply with the NonCommercial restriction if it applies."
5. **对星屿的落点**〔推断〕：同屏并排显示两张独立文件、各自署名，属于组合；把 ARASAAC 人物抠进 OpenMoji 情境图、给 ARASAAC 图符叠 OpenMoji 表情、或把两者拼成一张新 SVG，属于改编。ADR-0006 决定 10 的"目录隔离"必要但不充分，NC 政策需补一句"NC 素材不得与任何 BY-SA 素材合成为同一图像文件"。这是对 ADR-0006 的补充，不与其冲突。

### A3　图库与插画站：条款原文（均于 2026-09-24 读取）

- [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license)："Unsplash grants you an irrevocable, nonexclusive, worldwide copyright license to download, copy, modify, distribute, perform, and use images from Unsplash for free… This license does not include the right to compile images from Unsplash to replicate a similar or competing service."；"未修改"只出现在售卖限制："Images cannot be sold without significant modification"。
- [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/)："Don't redistribute or sell the photos and videos on other stock photo or wallpaper platforms."；"Identifiable people may not appear in a bad light or in a way that is offensive."
- 插画站 unDraw、Storyset、DrawKit 的原文见 §二.3。〔推断〕把文件提交进公开 git 仓库 = 任何人可单独下载、镜像、fork，很可能落入"distribute the assets in packs or otherwise"（unDraw）、"redistribute … on a stand-alone basis or as a compilation"（DrawKit）、"included … in a database, archive … collection … or library, for distribution"（Storyset §6）的定性。核查清单 §三.2 的结论（不入库）应扩展到这三家。

### A4　CAFE：两处 "CC BY" 都不是图像许可

- **论文许可**：LoBue & Thrasher（Front. Psychol. 5:1532，2015-01 刊出）文末："This is an open-access article distributed under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY)."——这是文章许可（[Europe PMC 全文接口 PMC4285011](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC4285011/fullTextXML)、[Frontiers 原文页](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.01532/full)）。
- **网站页脚**：Databrary 网站模板 [`site_footer.html`](https://github.com/databrary/www/blob/master/common/theme/templates/partial/site_footer.html) 写 "This work is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License"，是网页材料许可。数据集页脚另写 "Each dataset on Databrary represents an individual work owned by the party who contributed it. Use of Databrary is subject to the Terms & Conditions of Use and the Databrary Access Agreement."（[CAFE 卷页 Wayback 2024-03-07 快照](https://web.archive.org/web/20240307051323/https://nyu.databrary.org/volume/30)；线上卷页现为 JS 壳页）。
- **图像访问规则**：论文原文 "Faculty members can register with Databrary to become users and gain access to the photographs in the set… Once registered, faculty can grant graduate students, post-docs, and staff permission to access the set as well."；家长授权范围："Parents of the participating children signed a model release giving permission for the use of their photographs in research by the greater scientific community."
- **Databrary Access Agreement**（[databrary/policies · agreement.md](https://github.com/databrary/policies/blob/master/agreement.md)，"Version date: April 29, 2020"；网站 `about/agreement` 页以 `Source: ../policies/agreement.md` 引用该文件，见 [agreement.mdi](https://github.com/databrary/www/blob/master/databrary/input/pages/about/agreement/agreement.mdi)）：由 "an academic, research, government, or health entity" 为其 Authorized Investigators 签署；"All Institutions and Authorized Investigators agree to use Databrary and data obtained through it exclusively for the purpose of non-commercial scientific research or education."；Annex I 要求 "Respect the permissions research participants have given regarding data sharing" 与 "Secure data downloaded from Databrary with the highest safeguards…"（[agreement-annex-I.md](https://github.com/databrary/policies/blob/master/agreement-annex-I.md)）。
- 〔推断〕单人公益开发者没有机构背书，成不了 Authorized Investigator；即便成为，公开再分发也超出访问协议与家长授权范围。CAFE 可作为将来与机构合作做验证研究时申请使用的科研基准，不能当素材。

## 二、可用源全景表

### 二.1　判定口径

- **可直接入库**：许可允许再分发 + 修改 + 开源打包（核查清单"准入四问"第 1 问），按许可署名即可。
- **有条件入库**：许可允许，但须满足额外条件（NC 目录隔离、禁与 BY-SA 合成、逐件核出处、只提交预生成文件等）。
- **仅作风格参考**：许可允许在产品中使用，但不允许成包或单独再分发，〔推断〕随公开仓库分发即越界；只能由人看着参考画风，不入库。能否作为 AI 生成的参考输入，本表只列许可原文里的 AI 条款，合规结论归报告 10。
- **不可用**：限科研、限授权研究者、许可不明，或含真人肖像且无法满足 §三 的要求。

### 三.3　开源仓库与"可撤回同意"〔推断〕

- 法律要求：撤回同意后须删除（PIPL 第 15、47 条，见上表）。
- 技术现实（[GitHub 官方文档：Removing sensitive data from a repository](https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/keeping-your-account-and-data-secure/removing-sensitive-data-from-a-repository)）："You cannot remove sensitive data from other users' clones of your repository."；"If the commit that introduced the sensitive data exists in any forks, it will continue to be accessible there."；"If a fellow developer has a clone from before your rewrite, and after your rewrite simply runs `git pull` followed by `git push`, the sensitive data will return."；PR 中的缓存视图须 "contacting us through the GitHub Support portal" 才能移除，且 "GitHub Support won't remove non-sensitive data, and will only assist in the removal of sensitive data in cases where we determine that the risk can't be mitigated by rotating affected credentials."
- 项目自身约束：ADR-0006 决定 6 对 main 设了"禁强推"，改写历史须先手动关规则。
- 推断结论：儿童照片一旦进入公开仓库，撤回同意后的删除义务无法完全兑现。这是"一张儿童照片也不进仓库"的**独立理由**，与版权许可无关。

### 三.4　对星屿的落点

1. **仓库内零真人照片**（维持核查清单 §三.1–3.2 结论，理由补强为：科研库条款 + PIPL 敏感信息 + 肖像许可可解除 + git 不可删）。
2. **家长本地导入**是唯一可行的真人档路线。〔推断〕照片不离开设备、项目方不收集（ADR-0004），项目方不"自主决定处理目的、处理方式"（PIPL 第 73 条（一）对"个人信息处理者"的定义），家长为家庭事务使用落在第 72 条；二期做导入功能时，这一定性建议请执业律师确认一次。
3. **机构合作拍摄**（核查清单 §三.4，远期）若启动，至少要：监护人单独同意 + 专门处理规则（PIPL 第 29、31 条）+ 书面肖像许可写明期限（民法典第 1022 条）+ 撤回通道；且照片不宜进公开仓库、也不宜用 CC 这类不可撤回许可发布〔推断〕。

## 四、面孔表征证据

本节回答：自制卡通儿童面孔时，"画同龄人""画本族面孔"有没有证据撑腰。结论先行：**两条都只有弱证据，不构成硬约束**；一期对话中的两句概括需要改写（见 §四.3）。

### 四.1　own-race（本族面孔）效应

| 研究 | 任务类型 | 样本 | 结果（原文） | 来源 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Elfenbein & Ambady 2002，*Psychological Bulletin*（元分析） | **情绪识别**（跨文化） | 成人为主 | "Accuracy was higher when emotions were both expressed and recognized by members of the same national, ethnic, or regional group, suggesting an in-group advantage. This advantage was smaller for cultural groups with greater exposure to one another" | PMID 11931516（[E-utilities](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=11931516,21895562,32581859,31981278&retmode=xml)） |
| Segal et al. 2019，*J Exp Child Psychol* 182:102–113 | **情绪识别** | 儿童 69 人（6–10 岁）+ 成人 82 人，加拿大 | "the research with children is less clear; whereas some studies suggest better emotion recognition for own-race and own-culture faces, others have found no such relationship"；"South Asian participants, but not White participants, performed better when judging own-race faces… This finding only partially supports an own-race bias" | PMID 30818225（[E-utilities](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=30818225,26098913,25978656,36459504&retmode=xml)） |
| Wilson et al. 2011，*Q J Exp Psychol* | 身份匹配 | ASD 27、TD 47 名儿童 | "we found a typical advantage for recognizing own- over other-race faces in both the ASD and TD groups"；但 "a subgroup of ASD participants with impaired face recognition did not exhibit a significant own-race recognition advantage" | PMID 21895562（[E-utilities](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=11931516,21895562,32581859,31981278&retmode=xml)） |
| Chien et al. 2014，*Research in Autism Spectrum Disorders* | 身份辨别 | ASD 18 名（平均 7.5 岁）、TD 13 名，台湾 | "The TD children showed a significant own-race advantage… The ASD children did not exhibit such advantage at all." | DOI 10.1016/j.rasd.2014.08.005（摘要经 [OpenAlex](https://api.openalex.org/works?search=%22Autistic%20children%20do%20not%20exhibit%20an%20own-race%20advantage%22) 读取） |
| Hanley et al. 2020，*Developmental Science* | 身份识别 | 英国 ASD 16 / TD 16，日本 ASD 26 / TD 26 | ASD 组 "importantly they showed an ORA"；"There was considerable heterogeneity in the presence of the ORA in ASD and TD and also across cultures." | PMID 31981278（[E-utilities](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=11931516,21895562,32581859,31981278&retmode=xml)） |

读法：

- 情绪识别层面的 in-group 优势在成人元分析里存在，且随群体间接触增多而缩小；儿童研究结论不一致（Segal 2019 原文）。一期概括中的"小而脆弱"，"脆弱"可由上述原文支撑〔推断〕，"小"的量级本轮只读到摘要、未核到具体效应量。
- **"ASD 儿童不表现 own-race advantage"不能作一般结论**：Chien 2014 支持，Wilson 2011（多数 ASD 儿童有）与 Hanley 2020（英日两地 ASD 组均有）相反；而且三项全是**身份**识别任务，没有一项测的是情绪识别。

### 四.2　own-age（同龄面孔）效应

| 研究 | 任务类型 | 样本 | 结果（原文） | 来源 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Griffiths et al. 2015，*PLOS ONE* 10:e0125256 | 情绪标注（原型脸） | 5–8 岁 33 人、9–13 岁 70 人、成人 92 人（典型发育） | "There was no evidence that children or adults recognised expressions better on faces from their own age group… This suggests there is no substantial own-age advantage in children's emotion recognition." | PMID 25978656（[E-utilities](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=30818225,26098913,25978656,36459504&retmode=xml)） |
| Surian & van den Boomen 2022，*PLOS ONE* 17:e0278483 | 情绪标注（5 级强度） | 5–14 岁 152 人、成人 30 人（典型发育） | "children and adults have an age bias at low levels of intensity (25%)"；"both adults and children label most expressions best in adult rather than child faces, leading to an other-age bias in children and an own-age bias in adults"；并称 "to date the existence of an own-age bias for emotion recognition in children remains inconclusive" | PMID 36459504（[E-utilities](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=30818225,26098913,25978656,36459504&retmode=xml)）；[全文 PMC9718404](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9718404/fullTextXML) |
| Hauschild et al. 2020，*Frontiers in Psychiatry* | 情绪识别（DANVA-2 儿童脸/成人脸分测验） | 11–14 岁青少年 92 人（含 ASD） | "an own-age bias was evident, reflecting greater FER performance for child compared to adult faces, for all adolescents regardless of ASD status or symptoms. However, the strength of the observed own-age bias did not differ by ASD status or severity." | PMID 32581859（[E-utilities](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pubmed&id=11931516,21895562,32581859,31981278&retmode=xml)） |
| **Su et al. 2023**，*Psychology Research and Behavior Management* 16:4479–4490 | **干预**：同龄脸 vs 非同龄脸作教学材料，12 周、每周 2 次 | 24 名 ASD 儿童入组，分析 22 名（每组 11 人），平均年龄 10.6±1.2 / 10.8±1.9 岁 | "the own-age face intervention group gazed at children's faces significantly more often than before the intervention, gazed at children's angry faces significantly longer…; the other-age faces intervention group did not differ significantly"；结论："own-age faces as teaching materials can better promote the emotion recognition ability of children with ASD than other-age faces" | DOI 10.2147/PRBM.S427006；[PMC 全文](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10629357/)、[Europe PMC 全文接口](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10629357/fullTextXML) |

读法：

- **"own-age 在 ASD 情绪识别干预中有正向实证"——找到了具体论文，即 Su et al. 2023**，但证据很弱：①结果部分报告的全是**注视指标**（注视次数、时长），结论却外推到"emotion recognition ability"〔推断：结局与结论不对应〕；②分析样本 22 人、单一特教学校；③被试约 10–11 岁，高于星屿 4–8 岁的设计人设；④作者自述局限："The intervention materials in this study were not evaluated by children with ASD… longer-term effects have not been studied"。
- 典型发育儿童方面，Griffiths 2015（含 5–8 岁组）没有发现同龄优势，Surian 2022 发现儿童反而对成人脸标注得更好。Surian 2022 还指出一项 11–14 岁青少年研究 "it cannot be excluded that presentation order (all adult faces presented before all child faces) affected the reported bias"〔推断：所指应为 Hauschild 2020，年龄段一致〕。

### 四.3　对星屿素材设计的含义〔推断〕

1. 一期对话中的两句概括应改写为："own-race 优势在情绪识别层面有、但随接触而缩小，儿童结果不一致；ASD 相关研究全是身份识别任务且结论互相矛盾"；"own-age 用于 ASD 情绪识别干预只有一项小样本注视研究（Su 2023），典型发育儿童未见同龄优势"。
2. 这两条都**撑不起"只画同龄 / 只画本族"的硬规则**。更稳的做法是把面孔的年龄与族裔当作刺激变异的维度：卡通儿童脸为主、同时保留成人脸（家长真人示范本身就是成人脸），与报告 01 的"多情境、多面孔"对策、ADR-0002 的泛化原则一致。
3. 表情强度值得单独考虑：Surian 2022 发现面孔年龄的影响在低强度（25%）表情上最大，这与核查清单已有的"多强度变体"做法契合。
